EVIDENCE BEFORE CERTAINTY

Wallet tracing methodology and FAQs

Jeet Analyzer reconstructs observed Solana token activity within a bounded investigation. Its reports distinguish a wallet’s inventory, historical exits, later reacquisition and evidence-backed related-wallet exposure. Every conclusion must be read with its scope and coverage.

What a wallet trace examines

An investigation starts from the target token and selected wallet or wallet set. The engine collects available transaction history, classifies relevant transfers and sales, reconstructs inventory and compares it with a fresh target-token balance. The report retains evidence and limitations rather than filling missing history with assumptions.

A transfer to another wallet differs from a market sale. SOL funding differs from movement of the token under investigation. Related wallets are explored through supported relationship evidence and configured depth, wallet, time and provider budgets; a bounded graph is not a complete map of all possible connections.

How to read the exit statuses

Verified Out — VERIFIED_OUT
The available evidence supports an exit for the stated wallet or scope under the relevant coverage, inventory reconciliation and materiality rules. A small current balance alone is not enough. Read the wallet result separately from the cluster result: a wallet can be out while linked wallets retain exposure.
Re-entered — RE_ENTERED
A verified historical exit is followed by material reacquisition, with current holdings above the report’s threshold. The historical sale does not describe current exposure by itself.
Not Out — NOT_OUT
The result does not establish a current exit under its rules. Inspect holdings, retained exposure and evidence; the label is not a prediction of future selling.
Insufficient Data — INSUFFICIENT_DATA
The required coverage is missing or incomplete. Provider limits, exhausted budgets or unavailable history can prevent a stronger conclusion.
Unresolved — UNRESOLVED
Reconciliation or classification problems leave an exit uncertain. Unknown evidence is not converted into a clean bill of health.

Linked wallets and control-risk methodology

Relationship evidence may include direct target-token transfers, direct SOL funding, token-account preparation, non-market co-signing or delegated token authority. These are different evidence classes, not interchangeable proofs of ownership.

The current likely-control rule requires at least two independent evidence classes across at least two distinct transactions, combining a coordination/control signal with an economic/resource signal. Facts from a single transaction are not counted twice as independent confirmation. Delegated token authority remains relationship evidence rather than automatically becoming a control signal.

LIKELY_COMMON_CONTROL is a risk assessment, not proof of legal identity or beneficial ownership. The report preserves COMMON_CONTROL=NOT_PROVEN and legal identity NOT_ESTABLISHED. Funding, a shared counterparty or a visually close node cannot establish common ownership on its own.

What the tool cannot prove

  • It cannot guarantee discovery of every related wallet or complete historical coverage.
  • It cannot identify the human behind an address or prove beneficial ownership from links alone.
  • It cannot guarantee a token is safe, profitable or free of future selling.
  • It cannot treat an unavailable transaction or missing page as evidence that nothing happened.

Frequently asked questions

Does a seller’s empty wallet prove the tokens are gone?

No. Tokens may have moved to another wallet, and an apparently empty balance does not reconstruct the transaction history. Review transfers, reconciliation and the related-wallet scope.

Does “complete” mean the scan found everything?

No. It means the job reached a terminal state. Time windows, provider response limits, budgets and unresolved evidence still qualify the result. Read the coverage and receipt.

Are linked wallets owned by the same person?

Not necessarily. A link proves only the supported relationship. Even a likely-control assessment does not establish legal identity or common ownership.

Why can a historical exit and current re-entry both be true?

They describe different times. A wallet can exit, later reacquire the token and currently hold it. The report separates those stages instead of treating the old sale as a permanent status.

Does analyzing a wallet require signing a transaction?

The forensic investigation is read-only and does not sign or submit transactions. If you choose optional paid membership, its payment is a separate action.

Can I use the result as a trading guarantee?

No. Use the evidence and limitations to inform your own research. The tool does not predict prices or guarantee future behavior.
