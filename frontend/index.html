What is Jeet Analyzer?

Jeet Analyzer is a Solana wallet-tracing and linked-wallet analysis tool. It examines observed target-token movements, balances and transaction evidence to help answer whether a wallet sold, transferred, retained or reacquired tokens.

A visible sale is one event, not proof that an entire related-wallet cluster is out. We separate the root wallet’s status, linked-wallet exposure and the limits of the collected history.

Exit, re-entry and what remains unknown

Verified Out is a scoped conclusion that depends on sufficient history and inventory reconciliation. Re-entered means a verified historical exit was followed by material reacquisition and current holdings. Neither label proves that every potentially connected wallet has been found.

Missing pages, bounded scans, unresolved transfers or provider limits can leave a result INSUFFICIENT_DATA or UNRESOLVED. A finished job is not automatically a complete investigation.

Links are evidence, not identity

Direct token movement, funding and coordination can establish different kinds of links. A SOL transfer alone is not target-token movement or proof of common ownership. Control-risk assessments describe evidence patterns; COMMON_CONTROL=NOT_PROVEN remains distinct from an assessment of likely control.

Investigations are read-only. They do not sign or submit trades, identify a human owner or guarantee a token is safe. Optional membership payment is a separate action from forensic analysis.
